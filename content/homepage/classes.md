We teach three disciplines, to students of all ages and levels of experience.

##### Bharatanatyam

Open to all ages above 6 years. Available **both in person and online**.

##### Kalaripayattu

Open to all ages above 7 years. **In person only.**

##### Folk

Open to all ages above 7 years. **In person only.** 

##### **Drama**

Open to all above 8 years. **In person only.**
