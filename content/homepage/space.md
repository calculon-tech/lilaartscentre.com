Lila Arts Centre features a thoughtfully designed performance and practice space equipped for artists and learning. 

##### Performance Hall

An intimate venue perfect for dance performances, musical concerts, intimate theatre performances, and cultural events. 

##### Dance Studio

A spacious, well-lit studio with sprung wooden flooring designed for safe and comfortable practice. The space is equipped with full length mirrors, a sound system and air conditioning. 

##### Library

A small, curated collection of books, recordings, and resources on various forms of art and thinking.
